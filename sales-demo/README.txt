CHEKIN GUEST APP DEMO
=====================

A guided, clickable demo of the Chekin guest app. It works WITHOUT INTERNET.

START IT
--------
Mac:      double-click "Start demo (Mac).command".
          The first time, macOS may block it: right-click the file > Open > Open.
Windows:  double-click "Start demo (Windows).bat".
          If Windows SmartScreen appears: More info > Run anyway.

The demo opens in your browser at http://localhost:7900/
Keep the small terminal window open while you present (closing it stops the demo).
Best in Chrome or Edge.

THE FOUR USE CASES
------------------
1  Online check-in      property link, reservation link, one-click check-in, home, the whole group,
                        ID scan + liveness check, registered + next sale
2  Payments & deposit   one checkout (tourist tax, booking payment, security deposit),
                        tourist tax exemptions, deposit or protection, receipts
3  Upselling            extras list, early check-in, late check-out, add-ons, my extras
4  Keys & guidebook     keys locked until check-in is done, opening doors from the phone,
                        digital guidebook, the host's guidebook editor

HOW TO PRESENT
--------------
- Next / Back buttons, or the arrow keys. A presentation clicker works too.
- Keys 1-4 jump to a use case.
- Notes (N): talking points for each screen, with what to tap.
- Device: Phone + desktop, Phone, Tablet or Desktop, and the model next to it.
  No mockup shows the app straight on the computer screen, without a device frame.
  Your choice is remembered on this laptop.
- Full screen (F): shows only the app. Esc or F to come back.
- Restart (R): starts the current screen again.
- Every screen is clickable: tap buttons in the app to show the flow live.
  Nothing is saved: Restart or Next always brings back a clean screen.
